## 1. Word and length limits

- **Main text:** No formal word limit. PLOS ONE explicitly does not cap manuscript length; published Research Articles typically run 3,000–8,000 words.
- **Abstract:** ≤ 300 words, single paragraph, **unstructured**, **no citations**.
- **Title:** ≤ 250 characters; sentence case; specific and descriptive; do not begin with "A" or "The"; avoid specialist abbreviations.
- **Short title (running head):** ≤ 50 characters.
- **Methods:** No fixed limit; must be sufficient for full reproduction.

Source: https://journals.plos.org/plosone/s/submission-guidelines ; https://manusights.com/blog/plos-one-formatting-requirements

---

## 2. Required sections (default order)

1. Title
2. Authors and affiliations
3. Abstract (≤ 300 words, unstructured)
4. Introduction
5. Materials and Methods *(may be moved to after Discussion or before Results)*
6. Results *(can be combined with Discussion)*
7. Discussion
8. Conclusions *(optional; may be merged with Discussion)*
9. Acknowledgments
10. References
11. Figure legends
12. Tables
13. Supporting Information captions

Source: PLOS ONE Manuscript Organization (Submission Guidelines page).

## 3. References

- **Style:** PLOS numbered (Vancouver-derived).
- **In-text:** Square-bracket numbers in order of first appearance: `[1]`, `[2,3]`, `[4-7]`.
- **Reference list:** Numbered in order of first citation, not alphabetical. Include DOI for every reference that has one.
- **Author names:** Surname followed by initials, no periods between initials, separated by commas. List up to six authors; if more, list first six and add "et al."
- **Journal article example:**
  > Heber U. Astronomy. White dwarf mergers and the rebirth of luminous stars. Science. 2002;296: 2344–2345.
- **Book example:**
  > Angelou M. I Know Why the Caged Bird Sings. 1st ed. New York: Random House; 1969.
- **Newspaper example:**
  > Brantley B. Next Stop: An a Cappella Subway Journey. New York Times. 12 Dec 2016: C6.
- BibTeX style file: `plos2015.bst` (shipped with PLOS LaTeX template).

Source: https://paperpile.com/s/plos-one-citation-style/ ; PLOS ONE submission guidelines.

---

## 4. Figures

- **File format:** TIFF (raster) or EPS (vector).
- **Vector EPS generated by LaTeX is NOT accepted** — export from Adobe Illustrator or Inkscape.
- **Resolution:** 300–600 dpi (PLOS production tool NAAS validates).
- **Width:** Single column ≤ 13.2 cm; 1.5-column ≤ 19.05 cm; double-column up to journal max.
- **Color mode:** RGB. Colorblind-safe palettes encouraged.
- **Citation in text:** Abbreviated "Fig" — e.g. "Fig 1", "Fig 2"; "Figure 1" only at the start of a sentence.
- **Captions/legends:** Placed in main manuscript after References, in numerical order. Caption begins with figure label and a short bold title.
- **Submission:** Each figure as a separate file at submission.

Source: https://journals.plos.org/plosone/s/figures

---

## 5. Tables

- Editable text, not images.
- Cited as "Table 1", "Table 2".
- Placed after Figure legends in the main manuscript or supplied as separate editable files.
- Footnotes via superscript symbols below the table.

## 6. Mandatory statements

### Data Availability Statement (mandatory)
- Provide repository name + DOI / accession number / persistent identifier for every dataset underlying the findings.
- Restricted-access data: explain why and the procedure for an interested researcher to request it.
- Accession numbers also cited within the manuscript text where the data are first mentioned.
- If accession numbers are not available until acceptance, they must be provided at acceptance.
- PLOS strongly recommends domain-appropriate repositories (GenBank/EMBL/DDBJ for sequences; ArrayExpress/GEO for arrays; PRIDE for proteomics; figshare/Zenodo/Dryad for general data).

Source: https://journals.plos.org/plosone/s/data-availability

### Funding Statement (mandatory)
- Each source listed with grant number(s) and a description of the funder's role.
- If unfunded, use the verbatim sentence:
  > "The author(s) received no specific funding for this work."
- Entered in the financial-disclosure section of the submission system (NOT in the manuscript file).

Source: https://journals.plos.org/plosone/s/disclosure-of-funding-sources

### Competing Interests (mandatory)
- Full disclosure of all potential competing interests.
- Patents, patent applications, products in development or for market: full details (numbers, titles).

Source: https://journals.plos.org/plosone/s/competing-interests

### Author Contributions — CRediT (mandatory)
- At least one CRediT role per author, entered in the submission system.
- All authors must have reviewed, discussed, and agreed their individual contributions.
- Submitting author is responsible for completing the form.

Source: https://journals.plos.org/plosone/s/authorship

### Ethics statement (mandatory if applicable; required at submission since March 2023)
- **Human subjects:** IRB or equivalent ethics committee name, approval number, compliance with Belmont Report and/or Declaration of Helsinki, informed-consent procedure (or justification for waiver). Documentation must be uploaded at submission; absence triggers rejection without external review.
- **Animal research:** IACUC or equivalent name, permit/approval numbers, ARRIVE 2.0 reporting.
- **Unregulated animals / no committee required:** explicit statement and justification.

Sources: https://journals.plos.org/plosone/s/human-subjects-research ; https://journals.plos.org/plosone/s/animal-research

## 7. Generative-AI policy

See `ai_policy.md` for the disclosure template. Summary: AI tools cannot be authors; AI use must be disclosed in a dedicated Methods sub-section or in Acknowledgments; reviewers/editors must not upload submission content to AI platforms.

Source: https://journals.plos.org/plosone/s/ethical-publishing-practice

## 8. File formats

- **Initial submission:** DOCX, RTF, or PDF.
- **Acceptance / final submission:** LaTeX source (`.tex` + `.bib` + figures) OR DOCX. Multiple `.tex` files must be combined into a single `.tex`.
- **Open access:** All articles published under CC-BY 4.0; APC charged on acceptance.

---

## 9. Editorial policy

- Decisions based on **scientific validity and methodological rigor only** — not novelty or perceived significance.
- Replication studies, negative results, and null findings are explicitly in scope.
- Acceptance rate ≈ 50%; desk-rejection rate ≈ 20%.
- Common desk-rejection grounds: out of scope; obvious methodological problems; missing data-availability statement; missing ethics approvals; insufficient language quality.

Sources: https://journals.plos.org/plosone/s/criteria-for-publication ; https://journals.plos.org/plosone/s/what-we-publish

## 10. Reporting standards (apply where the study type matches)

| Study type | Reporting guideline |
|---|---|
| Randomized trial | CONSORT (checklist + flow diagram) |
| Systematic review / meta-analysis | PRISMA (checklist + flow diagram) |
| Observational epidemiology | STROBE |
| Animal research | ARRIVE 2.0 |
| Diagnostic accuracy | STARD |
| Qualitative research | SRQR or COREQ |

---

## 11. Preprints

Permitted and encouraged (bioRxiv, medRxiv, arXiv, etc.). Disclose preprint posting in the cover letter and on the manuscript title page if required.
